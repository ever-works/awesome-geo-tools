## Overview

Yext Scout is an AI visibility platform designed for enterprises with multiple locations. Unlike national-level AI visibility trackers, Scout breaks down AI brand monitoring by individual location, showing how each branch appears in AI search results.

## Features

- AI visibility by individual brand location
- Map showing visibility per location based on engine data from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity
- Tracks performance against 20 closest competitors per scan based on location and trends
- Sentiment analysis — identifies recurring themes in AI outputs related to your brand
- Citation tracking — shows which sources AI answers use
- Agents that perform actions across Yext's marketing tools (requires the full Yext platform)
- Connects to a broader suite of brand visibility tools
