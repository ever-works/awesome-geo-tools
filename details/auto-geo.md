## Overview

auto-geo is an open-source command-line tool that audits web pages for AI-citation readiness and measures whether major generative AI search engines cite a domain.

## Features

- Open-source CLI for AI-citation readiness auditing
- Measures citation by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok
- AI citation readiness assessment
- Domain-level citation measurement

## Pricing
