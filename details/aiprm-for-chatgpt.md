## Overview

AIPRM for ChatGPT is a community-driven prompt template platform that provides SEO-focused prompts for content creation, audits, and optimization within ChatGPT.

## Features

- Community prompt templates
- SEO content prompts
- Audit templates
- Prompt management
- ChatGPT integration
