## Overview

Petra Labs is an enterprise-grade GEO solution that combines AI visibility tracking software with hands-on consulting services. It is designed for large enterprises with complex LLM visibility needs across massive datasets and fierce competition.

## Features

- Singles out final citations in AI responses — identifies which sites actually appear in AI's final citations
- Tracks individual brand channels' appearance in AI responses
- Differentiates between brands and sub-brands — useful for companies with many products
- Granular analysis of AI data points for enterprise needs
- GEO consulting team builds strategy and executes with content marketing and digital PR
- White-glove service with dedicated support
