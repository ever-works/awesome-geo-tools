## Overview

Clearscope is a content optimization platform that has extended its capabilities into generative engine optimization (GEO). It is designed for content creators and SEO teams adapting to the GEO era, connecting content creation with AI citation tracking.

## Features

- Content Inventory — tracks pages created with Clearscope and connects them to AI Cited Pages view
- AI Cited Pages — closes the gap between created content and what drives LLM visibility results
- AI Draft — AI content creation tool supporting different brand voices, content formats, and outline customization
- Topic Exploration — topic clustering/fan-out approach beyond primary keyword focus
- Share of voice measurement grounded in SEO best practices
- LLM tracking limited to ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity
- Metered custom prompt tracking
