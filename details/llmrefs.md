## Overview

LLMrefs is an AI search visibility tracker for keyword-level monitoring. You import the keywords you want to own, and the platform automatically generates fan-out prompts based on real AI conversations, then runs those across major answer engines.

## Key Features

- Keyword-level AI visibility tracking
- Automatically generates fan-out prompts based on real AI conversations
- Runs prompts across major answer engines
- Calculates brand rankings, share of voice, average position, and citation counts per keyword
- Inspect source URLs cited in responses
- Filter performance by engine or country
- Export data in CSV or via API
- Weekly-renewed reporting
- Monthly prompt volume estimates
- Geo-targeting across 50+ countries and 20+ languages
- Unlimited projects
- Agency-oriented workspace features

## Limitations

- Visibility and citation analytics platform, not an attribution product
- Does not connect visibility to on-site conversion or revenue outcomes

## Best For

Teams that want keyword-based AI visibility tracking with citation and competitor data across multiple answer engines.
