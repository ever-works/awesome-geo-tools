## Overview

ZipTie is an LLM monitoring and visibility tool built for teams that need granular, deep-dive analysis of their brand's presence in AI-generated responses. It offers detailed insights beyond surface-level metrics, with filtering at the URL level (not just domain level), by query, and by AI platform.

## Features

- Descriptive, detailed insights into LLM visibility performance
- Granular filtering at URL level, query level, and AI platform level
- AI Success Score — an at-a-glance estimate based on tracked mentions, sentiment, and citation inclusion
- Indexation Audits (technical GEO audit) — analyzes URLs to detect issues preventing indexation by LLM bots
- In-platform content optimization tool — identifies questions your existing content could answer better, with suggestions for where to incorporate answers
- Identifies the most influential URLs targeting relevant queries
