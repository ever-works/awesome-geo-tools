## Overview

Similarweb is a web traffic data platform that has extended its capabilities into AI visibility tracking. Its AI Brand Visibility tool combines SEO and GEO data in a single view, making it useful for side-by-side tracking of traditional search performance and AI search presence.

## Features

- AI Brand Visibility tool — identifies top prompts driving traffic, top sources for a topic, and traffic distribution across AI channels
- GA4-type reports for crawler traffic and topic performance
- Tracks referral traffic from AI chatbots
- Identifies overall topic themes bringing the most traffic
- AI ads tracking
- Combines SEO and GEO data for competitor and industry benchmarking
