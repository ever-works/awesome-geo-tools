## Overview

GEO/AEO Tracker is an open-source, local-first AI visibility dashboard for tracking brand mentions across AI search platforms.

## Features

- Brand mention tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Overview, and Grok
- Visibility scoring
- Citation analysis
- Competitor battlecards
- BYOK (bring your own key)
- Self-hosted

## Pricing

Free, open-source, self-hosted ($0/month).
