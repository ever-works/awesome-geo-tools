## Overview

Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit is an add-on for existing Semrush users that combines AI visibility tracking with Semrush's trusted SEO platform. It allows users to manage SEO data and AI search tracking in one place, with actionability to move directly into content optimization, backlink analysis, site audits, and competitive research.

## Features

- Tracks brand mentions, prompts, citations, sentiment, and competitor visibility across major AI platforms
- Extensive prompt database: 317+ million prompts
- Tracks ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, and Perplexity
- Integrates with Semrush SEO tools (content optimization, backlink analysis, site audits, competitive research)
- Semrush integration with Zapier — connect with 10,000+ apps for workflow automation

## Pricing

- Available as an add-on for existing Semrush users
- Starting at $99/month per domain/sub-user
- Charges based on number of users
